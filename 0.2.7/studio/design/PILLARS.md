# Design pillars

## Terrain creates time

Building and digging must create understandable route and elevation consequences. Terrain edits are the strategic heart of the game, not decorative construction.

## Pressure has a physical read

The crystal should be understood through terrain-aware movement, depth, spread limits, and visible coverage. Water/channels, elevation, vegetation, and player-made terrain are all implementation-level inputs to its behavior.

## Strategy resolves in action

The long maze phase sets up a shorter combat phase. Direct weapons, enemies, spawn points, and crystal damage should reward preparation without replacing terrain strategy.

## A world worth routing through

Biome variation, rivers, caves, vegetation, towns, ruins, and topography provide navigational texture and tactical context. They must remain deterministic from the world seed where applicable.

## Legible, performant voxels

The orthographic/isometric view must make surface height, ramps, targets, crystal, and route barriers readable while preserving streaming performance. Visual quality claims require human verification, not only structural tests.
