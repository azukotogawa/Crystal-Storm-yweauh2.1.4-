# Crystal Storm — Alpha Visual Contract

## Terrain
The rendered world must faithfully represent the current terrain state.
F4/data agreement is not sufficient if the camera disagrees.

A terrain column must correctly render:
- full voxels
- arbitrary excavation depth
- exposed sides
- cardinal ramps
- corner ramps
- terrain beneath ramps

## Scale
Terrain is the reference scale.

Vegetation, enemies, player, items, buildings, settlements and other world
features must be visually proportionate to the terrain voxel scale.

Features must not default to tiny decorative objects approximately 1/5
the apparent scale of the terrain.

## Underground
Future caves must remain readable from the gameplay camera.

Future requirements:
- camera control appropriate for deep excavation
- visibility/occlusion solution for terrain above the player
- local underground lighting
- torches/light sources
- readable player position and excavated space

Do not implement these until surface terrain geometry is stable.

## Crystal
The crystal is not merely an enemy/spawn visual.

Future world presentation must support:
- crystal spreading across terrain
- conversion/infection of terrain
- infection of vegetation/wildlife
- crystal accumulating vertically
- crystal growing into mounds/volumes above terrain

The system should therefore remain compatible with terrain whose visual
appearance and height can change as crystal spreads.

CORNER RAMP

A corner ramp is NOT a V-shaped ramp.

A valid corner ramp exists only when:
- the low cell is adjacent to two full blocks of equal higher height;
- those supporting blocks meet at a corner;
- neither supporting direction is itself a ramp;
- the geometry improves the large-scale hexagonal/isometric silhouette.

Rendered geometry:
- one diagonal square vertical face on the low cell;
- one triangular top face;
- the two high edges meet the tops of the two supporting full blocks;
- the low cell has solid terrain beneath it;
- no hole beneath the corner;
- no second downward or upward slope;
- no extra triangular funnel/prism geometry.

Invalid:
- full block + cardinal ramp;
- cardinal ramp + cardinal ramp;
- isolated L-shaped geometry;
- two opposing slopes;
- generic two-cell corner;
- stacked/compound triangular wedges;
- any geometry that visually produces a V or diamond ramp.

Corner ramps should be uncommon. They exist to create the large-scale diagonal/hexagonal character of the terrain, not to replace ordinary terrain corners.
