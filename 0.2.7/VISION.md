Crystal Storm

Crystal Storm is an action roguelike where terrain is your primary weapon.

The player does not defend a base.

Instead, they reshape the landscape to delay an expanding crystalline corruption long enough to grow powerful enough to destroy it.

The crystal behaves like a natural disaster.

It spreads.

It pools.

It climbs.

It consumes plants, animals, ruins, and settlements.

Every terrain feature changes the crystal's path.

Every minute the player chooses between:

• buying time
• becoming stronger
• destroying crystal
• exploring

The player's goal is to eventually fight back into the heart of the corruption and destroy its source.
