Plains

	Base block: Dirt

	Terrain description: Dune like hills, literally just what a sand dune looks like but flatter and with grass

Steppe
	Top block: Grass
	Base block: Stone
	
	Terrarin description: Think iceland. Relitivly flat with jagged outcrops and hills 

Forest	
	Top block: Grass
	Base block: Dirt
		
	Terrain description: Overall flatter than plains and steppe but with sudden, steeper but small mounds and divets

Marsh
	Top block: Water/Grass
	Base block: Mud/Dirt

	Terrain description: Completely flat with large amounts of ponds/streams

Mountians

	Top block: Grass/Stone
	Base block: Stone

	Terrarin description: Full of steep hills and holes. Make these hills and holes highly angled. Current mountains are more like plains.
