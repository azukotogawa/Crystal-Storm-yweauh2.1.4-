# Agent checkpoint

## cs-content-daynight-torches

Night is a light curve on the locked sun, not a new sun. Torches are a placeable prop with a cheap local light. They do not change damage, HP, XP, cooldowns, hitboxes, spawns, crystal, or the day length constant.

### Conflicts with the task

- The task said torches should affect enemies or crystal if the notebook says so. `docs/PLAN-0.3-world-feel.md` and `studio/ART-STYLE.md` do not. Torches are light only.
- `studio/ART-STYLE.md` locks the sun at `(0.55, 0.82, 0.38)` and keeps unshaded terrain with soft face shade. Night multiplies ambient, fog, directional energy, and `terrain_day_gain`. It does not rotate the sun or add a second one. Torches do not cast shadow maps.
- `docs/GUIDE.md` says there is no day-night clock. The task asks for a noticeable night and says not to change day length. No day length existed. The new visual day is locked at 480 seconds. `scripts/verify_daynight_torches.gd` fails if that constant changes.
- The showcase route still logs day/night and torch as absent, so the speedrun does not wait out the night. This task did not rewrite that route.

### What shipped

- Day length 480s. The clock starts at noon and does not advance while loading, the pause menu, the map, or the skill tree is open. Midnight is about 4 minutes of play after the world is ready.
- Recorded night/day ambient ratio is 0.36. Noon keeps authored ambient 0.22 and directional energy 0.58. Midnight ambient is 0.0792 and directional energy is 0.1624. Noon terrain gain stays 1.0. Midnight terrain gain is 0.48, applied after corner darken so the soft shade remains.
- Hotbar 8 (inventory slot 7) starts with 4 torches. Slot 6 stays empty for the crafted wall. Right click places a torch. R toggles it. Left click, with the torch selected, picks it up. It is not a placeable block and it does not break blocks.
- At most 8 torches. Each is an `OmniLight3D` with range 6, shadows off, lit energy 1.15. The same eight positions add a local term in the terrain shader. A ninth place is refused.

### EYEBALL FOR COLE

- Start a new world and look at the ground in the first minute. It should still be the current daytime, with the same soft corner darkening and the sun shadow falling the same way (the sun vector does not turn).
- Keep playing about 4 minutes (the clock pauses on the loading screen, pause menu, map, and skill tree). The world should go clearly darker but stay readable: corners still darker than face centers, crystal still visible, not a black frame.
- Press 8. That slot starts with four torches. Right-click open ground in front of you. A short wood pole and a warm flame should appear, with a small pool of light about three columns across and no extra shadow map.
- Stand next to that torch and press R. The flame and the light go out. Press R again and they come back.
- With 8 still selected, left-click the torch. It leaves the world and the stack goes back up. A ninth torch will not place while eight are already out.
- Dig with the pick, swing the sword, and right-click a herb or bomb. Those should behave as before: only the pick breaks blocks, and those right-click items still do not place a terrain block.
