# Vision

Crystalstorm is an action-strategy game where terrain is the player’s primary strategic weapon. A growing crystal spreads across the landscape, pools over terrain, absorbs world features, and can produce hostile pressure. The player buys time by changing routes and height relationships, then spends that time gaining enough capability to remove the corruption’s sources.

The intended run arc is:

1. Explore a procedurally generated, topographically varied landscape.
2. Read the crystal’s possible routes and reshape terrain to delay or redirect it.
3. Use the resulting time for resources, world interaction, and combat readiness.
4. Destroy crystal spawn points, culminating in the origin/boss spawn.

This is direction, not a promise that every part is content-complete. The current prototype already includes terrain editing, crystal flow, spawn-point code, basic entities, combat, vegetation, towns, saves, and a map; their balance, content depth, presentation, and final progression are still under development.
